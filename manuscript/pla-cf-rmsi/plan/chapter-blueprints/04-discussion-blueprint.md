# Discussion blueprint

Target file: chapters/04-discussion.md
Minimum: 4500 non-whitespace characters and 6 prose paragraphs.

### Paragraph 1
- Role: meaning
- Main claim: For a lightweight prototype that may see more than one load mode, Run 1 wins the average and Run 2 wins the worst relative mode. The screen should report both.
- Evidence IDs: numerical record
- Forbidden content: do not recommend Run 1 as qualified for a structural part.

### Paragraph 2
- Role: literature placement of layer thickness
- Main claim: The higher mean tensile and flexural strengths at 0.1 mm agree in direction with thinner-layer gains reported for PLA and short-carbon-fiber PLA, without transferring those percentages.
- Evidence IDs: S3, S4, S5
- Forbidden content: do not claim the same percentage improvements.

### Paragraph 3
- Role: raster and speed
- Main claim: Mean tensile and flexural strengths are higher at 0° than at 90°, while mean compressive strength is nearly flat. A Taguchi study that let infill vary found speed and raster less influential than infill, so the present ranking applies only with infill fixed.
- Evidence IDs: S6, S12, S16
- Forbidden content: do not say 0° was proven parallel to the load axis.

### Paragraph 4
- Role: what equal weight does
- Main claim: Min-max scaling gives the 56.80 MPa flexural span the same 0–1 range as the 23.80 MPa compressive span. Equal weight is equality after that scaling. Other weights would move the score, as normalization and weighting studies already show.
- Evidence IDs: S16, S17, S15
- Forbidden content: do not present equal weight as mechanically neutral in newtons.

### Paragraph 5
- Role: relation to other multi-response methods
- Main claim: Grey-relational and Pareto studies already show that the best setting for one PLA property is not the best setting for the combined response. RMSI is a simpler declared screen, not a replacement for those methods.
- Evidence IDs: S13, S14, S15
- Forbidden content: do not claim RMSI outperforms GRA on an external benchmark. No such comparison was run.

### Paragraph 6
- Role: fiber context
- Main claim: The nine runs cannot show whether chopped fiber helped, because neat PLA was not printed. External papers disagree on which property improves.
- Evidence IDs: S7, S8, S9, S10
- Forbidden content: no specific-strength calculation from the manufacturer density.

### Paragraph 7
- Role: limits
- Main claim: One observation per cell, an unrecorded infill percentage, unrecorded specimen dimensions, no confirmation print, and L9 confounding of interactions keep the result inside this screen. A replicated follow-up is the appropriate next measurement, as an L9 mechanical screen has already been flagged as provisional without replication.
- Evidence IDs: S13
- Forbidden content: no planned date, no user instruction, no claim that the missing values are known.
