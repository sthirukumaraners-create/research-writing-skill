# Evidence map

Sources are open-access peer-reviewed journal articles from 2024–2026. Usable facts are limited to titles, abstracts, and Crossref bibliographic records. No claim may go beyond the usable fact. Manuscript prose must be written from scratch.

The abstract states the statistical limits of the screening design and calls the flexural and hardness campaigns provisional pending replication. | Single-response tuning leaves conflicts among properties, and an unreplicated L9 screen is not a confirmation experiment. | An equal-weight index can expose that conflict, but it should be reported as a screen with the same replication limit. | Introduction paragraph 5 and Discussion | Direct |

## Facts that must not be written

- No neat-PLA control was printed in the present nine-run record, so no reinforcement benefit is claimed.
- Infill percentage, specimen dimensions, nozzle diameter, and within-run replicates are absent from the record.
- ASTM D638, D695, and D790 are the named test methods. They are not added to the reference list because that list is restricted to open-access journal articles.
- No p-value, ANOVA percentage, or signal-to-noise optimum is calculated. Each run has one observation per response.
- Measured strengths are not replaced, smoothed, or supplemented.
