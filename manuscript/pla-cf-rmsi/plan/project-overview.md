## Research information

### Background

Fused-filament parts can be loaded in tension, compression, and flexure. A print setting selected for one strength can be weak in another. Grey relational analysis, desirability functions, and related aggregations change the winner when the normalization or the weights change.

### Purpose

Define a reproducible equal-weight Relative Multimode Strength Index for nine PLA-CF runs, rank those runs, and compare that rank with a worst-case maximin rank. The index is a screening tool for lightweight prototype settings inside this data set.

### Method

Bambu Lab PLA-CF, nozzle 220 °C, bed 55 °C, infill density held constant at an unrecorded percentage. Taguchi L9 on layer thickness (0.1, 0.2, 0.3 mm), printing speed (30, 45, 60 mm/s), and raster angle (0°, 45°, 90°). One tensile (ASTM D638), compressive (ASTM D695), and flexural (ASTM D790) strength per run. Min-max normalization, equal-weight mean scaled to 0–100, plus the minimum normalized score.
